PRIMUS Kickoff Meeting in Berlin

2026-02-10

On February 9, the kickoff meeting of the new project PRIMUS – Präsentation und Rechtssichere Implementierung digitaler Musikeditionen took place at the offices of FIZ Karlsruhe in Berlin. Representatives of the ongoing Edirom Online Reloaded project also participated in the meeting, marking the beginning of a productive exchange between the two projects.

While Edirom Online Reloaded focuses on the modernization and modularization of the Edirom Online software architecture, PRIMUS addresses the systematic modeling and implementation of legal aspects in digital music editions. PRIMUS also will analyze various legal constellations and model them explicitly in a Rights Expression Language (REL). These structured rights statements will then be integrated into the edition software Edirom Online, enabling differentiated presentation of research objects based on encoded rights information.

PRIMUS is carried out by three cooperating partner institutions, who jointly combine expertise in digital musicology, software engineering, and legal research.


Project partners

  • Universität Paderborn
  • FIZ Karlsruhe
  • Akademie der Wissenschaften und der Literatur | Mainz
