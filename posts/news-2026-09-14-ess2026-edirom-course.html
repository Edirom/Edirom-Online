Edirom Online Course at Edirom Summer School 2026

2026-09-14

Once again, a course on Edirom Online was offered as part of the Edirom Summer School 2026. This time, the teaching team included not only members of the Edirom Online Reloaded project but also Francesco Maccarini from the PRIMUS project.

The course took place on September 14th and 15th, 2026, at the Software Innovation Campus of Paderborn University.

A particular focus was placed on simplifying the Docker-based build process for Edirom Online. By the end of the course, all participants were able to view their own Edirom Online editions, including their own changes.
