PRIMUS at Zugang Gestalten! in Berlin

2026-10-07

The PRIMUS project presented a poster at this year's Zugang Gestalten! conference in Berlin (September 30 - October 2, 2026).

The poster reported on the project's goals and current status, covering the identification of legal rights in digital music editions, the definition of relevant usage scenarios, and the planned implementation in Edirom Online.
